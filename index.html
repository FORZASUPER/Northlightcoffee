Our Story

Slow coffee, made with care.

Northlight Coffee Co. is an independent coffee roaster and retailer founded in 2015. We source high-quality Arabica beans from small farms and cooperatives, roast them in small batches at our own roastery, and serve them fresh in our cafés and through our online shop.

Our mission is simple: to make excellent coffee easy to enjoy while treating farmers, staff and customers with respect. We are committed to sustainable sourcing, responsible packaging and fair prices, and we work closely with our partners to improve quality year after year.

Whether you are buying beans for home brewing or looking for a comfortable place to meet friends, Northlight aims to offer friendly service, reliable products and a genuine coffee experience.

Our Coffee Beans

Four origins, roasted in small batches.

Northlight offers a carefully selected range of Arabica coffee beans from Ethiopia, Colombia, Brazil and Guatemala. Each lot is chosen for its flavour profile, then roasted in small batches at our own roastery to preserve aroma and balance.

We offer light, medium and dark roasts, whole beans or ground to order, and single-origin and house-blend options. Every bag carries the roast date, origin and tasting notes, so customers can choose with confidence.

Our Customers

Families and weekend shoppers.

Who they are

Our main customer group is families and weekend shoppers. They typically visit our cafés at weekends, often with children, friends or relatives, and buy drinks, coffee beans and small gifts for home.

They value a comfortable, clean and child-friendly space, clear pricing and friendly service. Their main challenge is finding somewhere relaxed where everyone can choose a drink they like without feeling rushed.

How we meet their needs

We meet their needs by offering family-friendly seating, smaller and larger drink sizes, hot chocolate and decaf options, and takeaway cups for weekend outings. Our 250 g and 1 kg bean packs suit home brewing, and staff can recommend mild roasts and simple brewing methods.

We also keep weekend opening hours clear and provide product information that helps families compare prices and choose quickly. By focusing on families and weekend shoppers, we aim to make Northlight a regular weekend stop.

Why Choose Northlight

Quality, service and value.

Quality and sourcing

We choose high-quality Arabica beans from trusted farms and cooperatives, then roast them in small batches at our own roastery.
